How the free license check works

  1. Send the plumber's business name, exactly as shown on their quote, card, truck, or website.
  2. Tell us your town, and the license number if you have it (optional).
  3. We search New Jersey's public Master Plumber license records — and Home Improvement Contractor (13VH) records where they fit the job.
  4. We tell you what the record shows: credential type, number, record-holder name, status, and expiry date if shown — and the date we checked.

A license check verifies a public record on the date checked. It does not guarantee work quality, price, insurance, or who arrives at your home.
